🕹️ How do I play the game?

Move around:
ZQSD or Arrow keys to control your car like a boss! 🚗

Brake:
Press Space to stop — or drift like a pro 💨

Mute the game:
M if you need peace (or silence the madness 😅🎵)

4. Restart your browser 🚀
